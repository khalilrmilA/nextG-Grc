The European Compliance Challenge
The Challenge

European Regulatory Pressure is Accelerating

NIS2 enforcement became binding across all EU Member States in October 2024. DORA imposed new ICT resilience obligations on all EU financial entities from January 2025. The EU AI Act introduces layered obligations through 2026. GDPR fines have exceeded €4.5 billion since enforcement began. Organisations relying on spreadsheets and disconnected tools cannot sustain this pace of regulatory change.

One Platform. Full EU Regulatory Visibility.

ComplyZone cross-maps GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and the AI Act to your ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls automatically. Evidence collected once flows to all equivalent controls across every active framework. Vendor contracts are analysed by AI locally — without exposing data to any external service. Auditors receive scoped portal access with no manual document preparation required.

Why Organisations Struggle
01
Overlapping EU regulatory obligationsGDPR · NIS2 · DORA
GDPR, NIS2, and DORA share overlapping security and data governance requirements. Without cross-framework mapping, compliance teams duplicate assessment effort across every regulation.
02
Third-party supply chain risk unmanagedNIS2 · DORA
Both NIS2 and DORA impose documented third-party oversight obligations. Most organisations lack a structured process to assess, score, and monitor supplier compliance continuously.
03
Vendor contracts signed without adequate reviewGDPR · DORA
Legal and security teams lack automated tooling to detect missing DPA provisions, GDPR-incompatible data transfer clauses, or DORA ICT risk terms before contracts are executed.
04
Audit preparation consumes weeks of team time
Evidence is scattered across email, shared drives, and individual systems. Each internal and external audit requires weeks of manual collection — diverting resources from active risk reduction.
05
AI systems deployed without governance documentationEU AI Act
The EU AI Act requires risk classification, transparency disclosures, and human oversight documentation for AI systems. Most organisations have no framework in place to meet these obligations.
06
Regulatory deadlines missed without automated monitoring
Contract expiry dates, DPA review cycles, NIS2 incident reporting windows — without automated monitoring, critical compliance obligations fail silently and without warning.
