The Problem & Our Solution
The Challenge

GRC is Broken for Most Organizations

Security and compliance teams are drowning in spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual processes. Each framework lives in its own silo. Evidence collection is a nightmare. Audit prep takes weeks. Risks go untracked until they become incidents — and regulators are getting stricter every year.

One Platform. Total Visibility. Real Control.

ComplyZone replaces the chaos with a single, unified GRC command center. Risk registers, compliance assessments, audit workflows, policy management, and vendor risk — all connected, all visible, all automated. Your team spends less time on administration and more time on reducing real risk.

Why Organizations Struggle

Spreadsheet Overload

Risk registers in Excel, compliance gaps in Sheets, audit findings in email. No single source of truth, no real-time visibility.

Duplicate Effort Across Frameworks

ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, NIS2 — teams re-assess the same controls for every framework because nothing maps standards together.

Audit Prep Takes Weeks

Evidence is scattered, documents are outdated, and auditors ask for things that haven't been organized. Every audit feels like starting from zero.

Vendor Risk is Invisible

Third-party suppliers carry hidden risk. Without structured TPRM, organizations only discover vendor issues after a breach or contract failure.

Deadlines Missed Silently

Contract renewals, review cycles, policy attestations — without automated monitoring, critical compliance deadlines slip through the cracks.

Enterprise Tools Cost Too Much

Existing GRC platforms are complex, expensive, and built for large enterprises. Mid-market teams are left with no practical option.
