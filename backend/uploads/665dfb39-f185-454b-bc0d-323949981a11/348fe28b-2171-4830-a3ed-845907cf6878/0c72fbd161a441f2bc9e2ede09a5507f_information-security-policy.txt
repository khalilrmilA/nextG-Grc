INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Organization: Acme Corporation
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Reviewed: March 1, 2026
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

==============================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
==============================================================

This Information Security Policy establishes the framework for managing
information security across Acme Corporation. It applies to all employees,
contractors, vendors, and third parties who access, process, or handle
company information and systems.

The scope covers all information assets including:
- Digital data stored on company systems, cloud platforms, and portable media
- Physical documents containing sensitive business information
- Network infrastructure, hardware, and software applications
- Third-party systems that process Acme Corporation data

==============================================================
2. INFORMATION SECURITY GOVERNANCE
==============================================================

2.1 Information Security Management System (ISMS)
Acme Corporation maintains an Information Security Management System (ISMS)
aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The ISMS scope document defines the
boundaries and applicability of the security program.

2.2 Roles and Responsibilities
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO): Owns and maintains this policy,
  oversees the ISMS, and reports security status to executive leadership.
- IT Security Team: Implements technical controls, monitors systems, and
  responds to security incidents.
- All Employees: Must comply with this policy and complete annual security
  awareness training.
- Department Managers: Responsible for ensuring their teams adhere to this policy.
- Data Owners: Responsible for classifying data and approving access.

2.3 Information Security Committee
An Information Security Steering Committee meets quarterly to review security
risks, approve policy changes, and oversee the security program. Members include
the CISO, CTO, Legal Counsel, and department heads.

==============================================================
3. RISK MANAGEMENT
==============================================================

3.1 Risk Assessment Process
Acme Corporation conducts formal information security risk assessments annually
and when significant changes occur. The risk assessment process:
- Identifies information assets and their value
- Identifies threats and vulnerabilities
- Evaluates the likelihood and impact of risks
- Determines acceptable risk levels
- Selects appropriate risk treatment options

3.2 Risk Register
All identified risks are documented in the organizational Risk Register, which
includes risk owner, likelihood, impact, risk score, treatment plan, and
residual risk. The Risk Register is reviewed quarterly by the ISMS committee.

3.3 Statement of Applicability (SoA)
A Statement of Applicability documents all ISO 27001 Annex A controls,
indicating which are applicable to Acme Corporation and justifying any
exclusions. The SoA is reviewed annually and updated when controls change.

3.4 Risk Treatment
Risks may be treated by:
- Mitigating: Implementing controls to reduce likelihood or impact
- Accepting: Formally accepting risks within the risk appetite
- Transferring: Using insurance or third-party arrangements
- Avoiding: Ceasing the activity that creates the risk

==============================================================
4. ACCESS CONTROL
==============================================================

4.1 Access Management Policy
Access to information systems is granted on the principle of least privilege
and need-to-know. All access must be formally requested, approved by the
data owner, and reviewed quarterly.

4.2 User Account Management
- All users must have individual accounts; shared accounts are prohibited.
- User accounts are created upon HR onboarding approval.
- Access is revoked within 24 hours of employment termination.
- Privileged access (administrator accounts) requires additional approval
  and is subject to enhanced monitoring.

4.3 Authentication Requirements
- All systems require strong passwords of at least 12 characters.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for:
  - All remote access (VPN, cloud services)
  - Administrative and privileged accounts
  - Access to systems processing sensitive data
- Passwords must not be shared and must be changed every 90 days.

4.4 Remote Access
Remote access is permitted only through the company-approved VPN with MFA.
Remote workers must comply with the Remote Working Policy and ensure their
home environment meets minimum security requirements.

==============================================================
5. ASSET MANAGEMENT
==============================================================

5.1 Asset Inventory
All information assets are documented in the Asset Register, including
hardware, software, data assets, and services. Each asset has an assigned
owner responsible for its protection.

5.2 Information Classification
Information is classified into four categories:
- Public: Information approved for public release
- Internal: General business information for internal use only
- Confidential: Sensitive business information with restricted access
- Restricted: Highly sensitive data (PII, financial, health data)

5.3 Acceptable Use
Company assets must only be used for authorized business purposes. Personal
use is limited and must not interfere with business operations or security.
Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.

5.4 Clear Desk and Clear Screen
Employees must maintain a clear desk policy, securing physical documents
when not in use. Computer screens must be locked when unattended using
automatic screen lock set to 5 minutes of inactivity.

==============================================================
6. CRYPTOGRAPHY
==============================================================

6.1 Encryption Standards
Acme Corporation requires encryption for:
- Data in transit: TLS 1.2 or higher for all network communications
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption for all sensitive data storage
- Portable media: Full-disk encryption required on all laptops and USB drives
- Email: Encrypted email required for transmitting confidential data

6.2 Key Management
Cryptographic keys are managed according to the Key Management Procedure,
which covers key generation, storage, distribution, rotation, and destruction.
Encryption keys are rotated annually and upon personnel changes.

==============================================================
7. PHYSICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL SECURITY
==============================================================

7.1 Physical Security Perimeters
All data processing facilities are protected by physical security perimeters
including:
- Card-access controlled entry
- CCTV surveillance systems
- Security guard presence during business hours
- Visitor management with escort requirements

7.2 Equipment Security
- Servers and network equipment are housed in locked server rooms.
- Equipment must not leave company premises without authorization.
- Clear desk policy applies to all workstations.
- Unattended equipment must be secured.

7.3 Secure Disposal
All media containing sensitive data must be securely disposed of using
certified destruction methods. Hard drives are wiped using DoD 5220.22-M
standard or physically destroyed. Certificates of destruction are retained.

==============================================================
8. OPERATIONS SECURITY
==============================================================

8.1 Change Management
All changes to information systems follow the Change Management Procedure:
- Changes must be documented and approved before implementation
- Testing is performed in non-production environments
- Rollback plans are prepared for all significant changes
- Emergency changes are documented and reviewed post-implementation

8.2 Malware Protection
All endpoints and servers run approved anti-malware software with:
- Real-time scanning enabled at all times
- Signature updates applied automatically (minimum daily)
- Scheduled full system scans weekly
- Alerts generated and investigated for any detections

8.3 Backup and Recovery
Critical data is backed up according to the Backup Policy:
- Daily incremental backups
- Weekly full backups
- Monthly backups retained for 12 months
- Backups are encrypted and stored offsite
- Recovery tests performed quarterly

8.4 Logging and Monitoring
Security event logging is enabled on all systems. Logs are:
- Centralized in a SIEM system
- Retained for minimum 12 months
- Reviewed daily by the security operations team
- Protected from unauthorized modification

8.5 Vulnerability Management
Vulnerability scans are conducted monthly on all systems. Critical
vulnerabilities must be patched within 30 days of disclosure. High
severity within 60 days. All others within 90 days.

==============================================================
9. COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY
==============================================================

9.1 Network Security
Network infrastructure is protected by:
- Next-generation firewalls at all perimeter points
- Network segmentation separating production, development, and corporate networks
- Intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS)
- Web application firewalls for customer-facing applications

9.2 Data Transfer
Transfer of sensitive data must use secure, encrypted protocols.
Personal data may not be transmitted via unencrypted email. File
transfer must use approved secure file transfer tools only.

==============================================================
10. SUPPLIER AND THIRD-PARTY RELATIONSHIPS
==============================================================

10.1 Supplier Security Assessment
All third-party suppliers with access to company data or systems are
assessed before engagement and annually thereafter. Assessments cover
security controls, certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2), and data handling practices.

10.2 Contracts and Agreements
All supplier contracts must include:
- Data Processing Agreements (DPA) for personal data handling
- Security requirements and right-to-audit clauses
- Incident notification obligations (within 24 hours)
- Data return and deletion requirements at contract end

10.3 Supply Chain Security
Technology supply chain risks are assessed and managed. Software
components are reviewed for known vulnerabilities. Critical suppliers
are subject to enhanced due diligence.

==============================================================
11. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
==============================================================

11.1 Incident Response
Acme Corporation maintains an Incident Response Plan. All security
incidents must be reported immediately to the IT Security team via
the security incident hotline or security@acme.com.

11.2 Incident Classification
- Critical: Active breach, ransomware, data exfiltration in progress
- High: Confirmed malware, unauthorized access, significant data loss
- Medium: Suspected compromise, policy violation with impact
- Low: Security anomalies, policy violations without confirmed impact

11.3 Incident Reporting
- Internal reporting: Immediately upon discovery
- Management notification: Within 1 hour for Critical/High incidents
- Regulatory notification: Within 72 hours where legally required (GDPR)
- Customer notification: As required by contracts and regulations

11.4 Post-Incident Review
All significant incidents are subject to post-incident review within
5 business days. Lessons learned are documented and used to improve
security controls and procedures.

==============================================================
12. BUSINESS CONTINUITY
==============================================================

12.1 Business Continuity Planning
Acme Corporation maintains a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and
Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP). Plans are tested annually through
tabletop exercises and technical recovery tests.

12.2 Recovery Objectives
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours for critical systems
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour for critical data

==============================================================
13. COMPLIANCE
==============================================================

13.1 Legal and Regulatory Compliance
Acme Corporation complies with all applicable laws and regulations
including GDPR, CCPA, and industry-specific requirements. Legal
requirements are tracked and reviewed quarterly.

13.2 Intellectual Property
All software is properly licensed. Open-source software usage is
documented and approved. Unauthorized copying or distribution of
copyrighted material is prohibited.

13.3 Privacy and Personal Data
Personal data is processed in accordance with the Privacy Policy
and applicable data protection regulations. Data Subject Rights
requests are handled within legally required timeframes.

13.4 Internal Audit
The ISMS is subject to internal audit annually. Findings are
reported to management and tracked to resolution. External
certification audits are conducted every three years.

==============================================================
14. SECURITY AWARENESS AND TRAINING
==============================================================

All employees must complete:
- Security awareness training upon onboarding
- Annual refresher security training
- Role-specific training for IT staff and privileged users
- Phishing simulation exercises quarterly

Training completion is tracked and non-compliance reported to HR.

==============================================================
15. POLICY REVIEW
==============================================================

This policy is reviewed annually or following significant changes
to the business, technology, or threat landscape. The CISO is
responsible for maintaining this policy. All updates require
approval from the Information Security Committee.

Policy violations may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination of employment or contract.
