The Compliance Challenge
The Challenge

Regulatory Pressure Keeps Accelerating

NIS2 enforcement began October 2024. DORA applies to all EU financial entities from January 2025. The AI Act introduces new compliance obligations through 2026. GDPR fines exceeded €4.5 billion since 2018. Teams managing compliance across spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual audits cannot keep pace.

One Platform. Full Coverage.

GRCEye maps GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and the AI Act to your existing ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls — automatically. Evidence flows across frameworks. Auditors have a dedicated portal. Third-party suppliers are risk-scored and contract-reviewed by AI. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Why Organisations Struggle

Overlapping Regulations

GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and the AI Act share obligations. Without cross-mapping, teams duplicate effort across every framework audit.

Third-Party Supply Chain Risk

NIS2 and DORA mandate supply chain risk oversight. Most organisations have no structured process to assess, score, and monitor supplier compliance.

Audit Preparation Takes Weeks

Evidence scattered across email threads and shared drives. Every audit feels like starting from zero, stalling the business for weeks.

Risky Vendor Contracts

Legal and security teams lack tools to detect GDPR-incompatible clauses, missing DPA provisions, or ICT risk terms before contracts are signed.

Policy Gaps at Audit Time

Policies in shared drives with no version history, no attestation records, and no link to the framework controls they are meant to satisfy.

No Board-Ready Risk Reporting

Heat maps and spreadsheets fail to translate risk exposure into the financial language boards need to make informed investment decisions.
