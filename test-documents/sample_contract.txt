1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
================================================================

The Service Provider agrees to provide the following cloud infrastructure
and managed security services to the Client:

1.1 Cloud Hosting Services
    - Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) infrastructure
    - 99.9% uptime SLA guarantee
    - 24/7 monitoring and support
    - Auto-scaling and load balancing

1.2 Data Storage and Backup
    - Up to 50 TB of encrypted storage
    - Daily automated backups with 90-day retention
    - Geo-redundant storage across 3 regions
    - Point-in-time recovery capabilities

1.3 Security Services
    - Web Application Firewall (WAF)
    - DDoS protection up to 100 Gbps
    - Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
    - Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
    - Vulnerability scanning (weekly)

================================================================
2. DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY
================================================================

2.1 Data Processing Agreement
    Service Provider acknowledges the processing of personal data on
    behalf of the Client and agrees to comply with applicable data
    protection laws, including GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA where applicable.

2.2 Data Encryption
    - All data at rest: AES-256 encryption
    - All data in transit: TLS 1.3 minimum
    - Encryption keys managed via AWS KMS

2.3 Data Residency
    Client data shall be stored exclusively in data centers located
    within the European Union or United States, as specified by Client.

2.4 Data Breach Notification
    Service Provider shall notify Client of any confirmed data breach
    within 24 hours of discovery. Notification shall include:
    - Nature of the breach
    - Categories of affected data
    - Number of affected records
    - Mitigation measures taken

2.5 Sub-processors
    Service Provider may engage sub-processors with prior written
    consent from Client. Current sub-processors: AWS, Cloudflare, Datadog.

3. SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
================================================================

3.1 Certifications
    Service Provider maintains the following certifications:
    - ISO/IEC 27001:2022
    - SOC 2 Type II (annual audit)
    - PCI DSS Level 1
    - HIPAA compliance (BAA available upon request)

3.2 Security Controls
    - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all administrative access
    - Role-based access control (RBAC)
    - Quarterly security assessments
    - Annual third-party penetration testing
    - Continuous vulnerability management

3.3 Incident Response
    Service Provider maintains a documented Incident Response Plan
    with the following response time commitments:
    - Critical incidents: 15 minutes
    - High severity: 1 hour
    - Medium severity: 4 hours
    - Low severity: 24 hours

3.4 Audit Rights
    Client has the right to conduct annual security audits with
    30 days prior written notice. Audits may include:
    - Review of security policies and procedures
    - SOC 2 report review
    - Penetration testing reports review

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
================================================================

4.1 Uptime Guarantee
    Service Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime (maximum
    43.2 minutes of downtime per month).

4.2 Service Credits
    In the event of SLA breach:
    - 99.0% - 99.9%: 10% service credit
    - 95.0% - 99.0%: 25% service credit
    - Below 95.0%: 50% service credit

4.3 Response Times
    - P1 (Critical): 15 minutes
    - P2 (High): 1 hour
    - P3 (Medium): 4 hours
    - P4 (Low): 1 business day
