The Challenge

European Regulatory Pressure is Accelerating

NIS2 enforcement began October 2024. DORA applies to all EU financial entities from January 2025. The AI Act introduces new compliance obligations in layers through 2026. GDPR fines exceeded €4.5 billion since 2018. Teams managing compliance across spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual audits cannot keep pace.

One Platform. Full EU Regulatory Coverage.

ComplyZone maps GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and the AI Act to your existing ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls — automatically. Evidence flows across frameworks. Auditors have a dedicated portal. Third-party suppliers are risk-scored and contract-reviewed by AI. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Why European Organisations Struggle

Overlapping EU Regulations

GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and the AI Act share obligations. Without cross-mapping, teams duplicate effort across every framework audit.

Third-Party Supply Chain Risk

NIS2 and DORA mandate supply chain risk oversight. Most vendors have no structured process to assess, score, and monitor supplier compliance.

Audit Preparation Takes Weeks

Evidence scattered across email threads and shared drives. Every internal and external audit feels like starting from zero, stalling business.

Risky Vendor Contracts Unsigned

Legal and security teams lack automated tools to detect GDPR-incompatible clauses, missing DPA provisions, or ICT risk terms before signing.

Regulatory Deadlines Missed

Contract renewals, DPA reviews, NIS2 incident reporting windows — without automated monitoring, critical obligations slip without warning.

AI Tools Without Governance

The EU AI Act demands documentation, risk classification, and oversight of AI systems. Most teams have no framework to prove compliant AI use.
