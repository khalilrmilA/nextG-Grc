ComplyZone is a unified GRC platform that connects risk management, multi-framework compliance, third-party vendor oversight, audit operations, and policy governance — in a single, AI-powered workspace where every team works from the same data.

Most compliance programs are held together by spreadsheets, email threads, and the institutional memory of whoever set them up. When an auditor requests evidence, it takes days to assemble. When a regulator asks about a supplier's data processing posture, someone has to dig through contracts that were signed months ago. When the board wants to understand risk exposure, the answer is a heat map that tells them nothing they can act on.

ComplyZone was built to replace that reality. It brings 76 compliance frameworks, a full risk register with formal scoring and acceptance workflows, an AI-powered contract intelligence engine, a dedicated auditor portal, and a real-time compliance dashboard into a single platform. Data entered anywhere flows everywhere — a control assessment in ISO 27001 automatically updates your GDPR and NIS2 coverage. Evidence uploaded once is shared across every mapped framework. A vendor contract uploaded today is reviewed by AI before it is signed.

The result is a compliance program that runs on evidence, not effort. Audit cycles that shrink from weeks to days. Vendor risk that is documented, scored, and defensible. And five automated PDF reports — Executive, Risk, Compliance, Vendor, and Audit — ready for any audience with a single click.

Core Value Propositions
  • One platform, every framework. ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, GDPR, NIS2, DORA, PCI DSS, and 69 more — cross-mapped, gap-analysed, and scored in real time.
  • Evidence collected once. Used everywhere. Cross-framework mapping ensures evidence uploaded to one control automatically appears on all equivalent controls in every active assessment.
  • AI that stays inside your infrastructure. Contract review, gap analysis, policy drafting, and vendor prescreening are powered by a self-hosted LLM — no external API, no data egress.
  • Custom frameworks built in minutes. Clone any of the 76 built-in frameworks into a fully editable tenant-owned copy, or build a new one from scratch with AI-assisted control generation.
  • Vendor contracts reviewed before they are signed. AI analyses clauses against GDPR DPA requirements, DORA ICT obligations, and your risk policy, and proposes redlines in minutes.

Platform Capabilities

Everything Your GRC Team Needs. Nothing They Do Not.

ComplyZone is structured around seven integrated modules. Each solves a specific operational problem, and each shares the same data layer — so actions in one module are immediately visible across all others.

Risk Intelligence

Quantified

Moves risk management beyond colour-coded heat maps into a structured, traceable register that connects directly to compliance controls.

  • 5×5 risk register with likelihood × impact auto-severity scoring (Low → Critical)
  • Formal acceptance workflow — justification, designated approver, expiry tracking
  • Linked to compliance — risks visible across framework assessments for full context
  • Deadline monitoring — automatic alerts when risk reviews become overdue
  • Risk heatmap — visual 5×5 grid overview of the full risk register by severity

Compliance Management

AI-Powered

Manages multi-framework compliance at a scale that manual processes cannot match. Evidence collected once flows everywhere it is needed.

  • 76 built-in frameworks — clone any into a tenant-owned, fully editable copy
  • Cross-framework evidence propagation — upload once, shared to all mapped controls
  • AI gap analysis — batch control verdicts: Compliant / Partial / Not Covered
  • Custom framework builder — AI-assisted control generation from scratch
  • Compliance score dashboard — real-time percentage per active assessment

Third-Party Risk & Contract Intelligence

TPRM

Structured supplier oversight from initial prescreen through contract execution — satisfying NIS2 supply chain and DORA ICT third-party obligations.

  • AI prescreen questionnaire — 15 questions analysed before the vendor record is created
  • Conversational contract review — AI queries any clause; detects GDPR DPA gaps and DORA ICT terms
  • CISO approval workflow — contracts cannot proceed without sign-off
  • 6-dimension risk cartography — automated heatmap scoring with risk tier multipliers
  • Expiry monitoring — automated alerts at 90 / 60 / 30 / 7 days

Audit Operations

Full Lifecycle

Reduces audit preparation from weeks to days. Auditors work in a scoped portal with no access to unrelated data.

  • Full audit lifecycle — Planned → In Progress → Completed with milestone tracking
  • Dedicated auditor portal — scoped to assigned assessments only, enforced server-side
  • Findings tracker — severity, remediation owner, management response, resolution notes
  • Stakeholder share links — expirable tokens for external evidence submission, no login required
  • PDF audit report generation — automated from findings and evidence records

Policy & Document Governance

29 Doc Types

Eliminates the blank-page problem. Policies are drafted by AI, versioned, attested to by employees, and published directly to auditors.

  • AI document generator — 29 types across Foundation, Security, Procedures, and Plans
  • Full version history — Draft → Under Review → Approved lifecycle with rollback
  • Employee attestation — formal acknowledgement workflow with completion tracking
  • Control linkage — policies tied to specific framework controls for traceability
  • Auditor publishing — approved policies flow automatically to the auditor portal

Reporting & Executive Dashboard

5 PDF Types

Turns live platform data into board-ready reports and executive dashboards without manual assembly or data export gymnastics.

  • 5 automated PDF reports — Executive, Risk, Compliance, Vendor, and Audit summaries generated server-side
  • KPI dashboard — real-time compliance scores, open risks, audit status, and vendor tier breakdown
  • Framework overview — per-assessment score, control coverage, and gap count at a glance
  • CSV export — risks, vendors, assets, audits, policies, and compliance data on demand
  • CSV import — bulk load risks, vendors, and assets with row-level error reporting

AI That Runs Inside Your Infrastructure — Not Someone Else's Cloud

Every AI capability in ComplyZone — gap analysis, contract review, policy drafting, vendor prescreening, framework building — is powered by a self-hosted LLM (Ollama). No contract text, no personal data, and no compliance information is ever transmitted to an external AI provider. Every AI output is logged and auditable, meeting EU AI Act transparency requirements and GDPR Article 28 data minimisation obligations.

Use Cases & Business Benefits

Real Problems. Measurable Outcomes.

These are not hypothetical scenarios. They are the daily operational challenges ComplyZone was built to solve — and the outcomes organisations see when they replace fragmented tooling with a unified platform.

01
Preparing for an ISO 27001 Certification Audit
CISO · Compliance Team
"Our last audit took three months of preparation. Evidence was scattered, controls were undocumented, and the auditor kept asking for things we couldn't find."
ComplyZone maps all 93 ISO 27001 Annex A controls to a single assessment. Evidence uploaded per control is automatically shared to equivalent NIS2 and GDPR controls. The mandatory document checklist tracks what is uploaded and what is still missing. The auditor receives a dedicated scoped portal — no manual document preparation, no email threads.
→

Audit preparation reduced from weeks to days. Full traceability from control to gap to remediation action. Evidence gap visible at a glance before the auditor arrives.

02
NIS2 Supply Chain Risk Oversight for a Critical Sector Operator
Risk Manager · Procurement
"We have 40 active vendor contracts. NIS2 requires us to document their security posture and oversight processes. We had none of that in writing."
Before a vendor is created, ComplyZone runs a 15-question AI prescreen and returns a risk score, concerns, and red flags. Contracts are uploaded and reviewed by AI — GDPR DPA gaps, DORA ICT terms, and liability clauses are flagged automatically. The CISO approves before execution. Risk cartography scores each supplier across 6 dimensions continuously.
→

Complete, auditable supplier risk register aligned with NIS2 Article 21. Every contract reviewed for GDPR DPA compliance before signing. Expiry alerts prevent supplier gaps from going unnoticed.

03
Building a Defensible Policy Library Under Audit Pressure
Compliance Officer · CISO · HR
"Our auditor asked for attestation records on 14 policies. We had drafts in a shared drive, no version history, and no proof that anyone had read them."
ComplyZone's AI document generator produces any of 29 policy and procedure types — Information Security Policy, BCP, Data Classification, Incident Response Plan and more — in your organisation's context. Each document moves through Draft → Under Review → Approved with full version history. Once approved, employees formally attest to it. The auditor sees approved policies and attestation records directly in their scoped portal without any manual handoff.
→

Complete, versioned policy library linked to framework controls. Attestation records available instantly during audit. New policies drafted in minutes, not weeks — with AI doing the blank-page work.
