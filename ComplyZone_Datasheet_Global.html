The GRC Challenge — & How We Solve It

Modern GRC Is Broken — And the Stakes Have Never Been Higher

Security and compliance teams are overwhelmed by sprawling regulation, fragmented tooling, and manual processes. Each framework lives in its own silo. Evidence collection is a repetitive nightmare. Vendor contracts are signed without adequate security review. Audit preparation consumes weeks. And regulators — from the EU's NIS2 to SEC Cybersecurity Rules — are demanding faster, more documented responses than ever.

ComplyZone: One Unified Platform That Actually Solves It

ComplyZone replaces the chaos with a single, connected GRC command centre. Risk registers, compliance assessments, audit workflows, vendor risk, policy governance — all share the same data model, so evidence collected for one framework flows everywhere it is needed. AI-powered contract review, gap analysis, and policy generation happen locally — your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Why Teams Struggle Without ComplyZone
01

Duplicate Compliance Effort

Teams reassess the same controls for ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, and NIS2 independently — because nothing maps standards together.

02

Contracts Signed Blind

No automated tooling to detect missing DPA clauses, GDPR-incompatible transfer terms, or ICT risk provisions before signature.

03

Vendor Risk Is Invisible

Supply chain suppliers carry hidden risk. NIS2 and DORA mandate documented oversight — most teams have no structured process.

04

Audit Prep Takes Weeks

Evidence scattered across drives and email threads. Each audit cycle forces teams to restart a slow, painful collection process.

05

Risk Reported, Not Quantified

Red/amber/green heat maps don't satisfy boards or regulators. Real risk decisions require financial loss exposure in monetary terms.

06

AI Without Governance

AI tools deployed without risk classification, oversight documentation, or audit trails — creating regulatory and reputational exposure.
