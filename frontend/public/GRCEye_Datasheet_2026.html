The Compliance Challenge
The Challenge

Regulatory Pressure Keeps Accelerating

NIS2 enforcement began October 2024. DORA applies to all EU financial entities from January 2025. The AI Act introduces new compliance obligations through 2026. In the Gulf, NCA, SAMA and SDAIA requirements keep expanding. Teams managing compliance across spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual audits cannot keep pace.

One Platform. Full Coverage.

GRC Eye cross-maps GDPR, NIS2, DORA, the AI Act and the Saudi NCA frameworks to your ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls. Evidence is shared across frameworks. Auditors have a dedicated portal. Suppliers are risk-scored and contracts reviewed by AI. Need data to stay in-house? Run the private edition on your own infrastructure.

Why Organisations Struggle

Overlapping Regulations

GDPR, NIS2, DORA, NCA ECC and ISO 27001 share obligations. Without cross-mapping, teams duplicate effort across every framework audit.

Third-Party Supply Chain Risk

NIS2 and DORA mandate supply chain risk oversight. Most organisations have no structured process to assess, score, and monitor supplier compliance.

Audit Preparation Takes Weeks

Evidence scattered across email threads and shared drives. Every audit feels like starting from zero, stalling the business for weeks.

Risky Vendor Contracts

Legal and security teams lack tools to detect GDPR-incompatible clauses, missing DPA provisions, or ICT risk terms before contracts are signed.

Policy Gaps at Audit Time

Policies in shared drives with no version history, no acknowledgement records, and no link to the framework controls they are meant to satisfy.

No Board-Ready Risk Reporting

Spreadsheets and static heat maps fail to give boards a clear, current view of risk exposure, compliance posture and remediation progress.

Third-Party Risk · TPRM

Supplier & Vendor Risk Management

NIS2 and DORA require documented third-party risk oversight. GRC Eye covers the full TPRM workflow — from vendor registration to contract signature and expiry monitoring.

  • AI prescreen questionnaire — 15 risk questions analysed automatically
  • Vendor risk cartography heatmap
  • Vendor collaboration portal — tokenized access, message thread, CISO + vendor e-signature
  • Contract expiry alerts at 90 / 60 / 30 / 7 days
AI Contract Intelligence

AI Contract Review

Upload a vendor contract and GRC Eye's AI reviews its clauses against GDPR DPA requirements, NIS2 / DORA ICT obligations and your risk policy — flagging gaps before you sign.

  • Structured AI review report: summary, key findings, recommendations
